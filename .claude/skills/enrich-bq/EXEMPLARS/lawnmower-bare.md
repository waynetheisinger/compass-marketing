# Exemplar — Cordless lawnmower (bare tool)

A worked example of B&Q-listing prose for a hierarchy: **LAWN_MOWER_BARE**
(PIM_13202, Core_Product type 12396).

Use this as a voice anchor when rewriting. **Lift the voice, not the
phrasing.** Your rewrite should differ at sentence level from this example
and from anything on MowDirect — same voice, same product truths, fresh
copy.

## Worked example — SBS460CLM (Spectrum 40V 46cm cordless lawnmower, bare tool)

### Product title (≤130 chars, no em-dash, no degree symbol)

```
Spectrum SBS460CLM 40V Cordless Lawnmower 46cm Cutting Deck Bare Tool
```

(69 chars — well inside the cap.)

### Marketing description (2-3 short paragraphs)

```
Mow lawns up to 800m² without lugging a cable or filling a petrol tank.
The Spectrum SBS460CLM runs on the same 40V battery as the rest of the
Spectrum cordless range, so one charge can move between mower, hedge
trimmer and blower without a second-tool fuss.

The 46cm steel cutting deck handles longer grass and damp mornings the
plastic mowers don't. Seven-position height adjustment runs from 20mm
fine finish to 75mm long-grass reset, and the 55-litre grass collector
holds enough to cross most front lawns before you stop to empty it.

Five-year warranty. Battery and charger sold separately — pair with any
Spectrum 40V battery from 2.0Ah upwards.
```

### Feature bullets (4-6, ≤12 words each, one spec per bullet)

```
- Covers up to 800m² per charge with the 40V battery system
- 46cm steel cutting deck shrugs off long grass and damp mornings
- Seven cutting heights from 20mm fine finish to 75mm long-grass reset
- 55-litre grass box clears most front lawns between empties
- Shares 40V batteries with the full Spectrum cordless range
- Backed by a five-year warranty as standard
```

### Why this works as a B&Q rewrite

- **Opens with the gardener's outcome** ("Mow lawns up to 800m² without lugging a cable") — not what the product is.
- **Specifics in every sentence**: 800m², 40V, 46cm, 20mm-75mm, 55 litres, 5 years. No fluff adjectives.
- **No banned moves**: no "B&Q", no "click and collect", no "MowDirect", no price, no Americanisms (petrol not gas).
- **Distinctive lead** vs. MowDirect's likely version. If MowDirect leads on the battery system, this version leads on the area covered.
- **British English** throughout.
- **No restricted chars**: no em-dash, no en-dash, no ×, no °.

### Counter-examples (what not to write)

✗ **"The SBS460CLM is a revolutionary cordless lawnmower with cutting-edge
   battery technology."** — fluff adjectives, "is a", reads like spec sheet.

✗ **"Available at B&Q with click and collect or home delivery."** —
   banned (mentions B&Q + click-and-collect).

✗ **"This game-changing mower from MowDirect will transform your garden."**
   — fluff + mentions MowDirect + makes a vague promise.

✗ **"Cuts grass at speeds up to 3,000 RPM."** — speed is a feature, not a
   benefit. Translate to outcome: "spins fast enough for a clean finish
   without grass shred."

## Building the same shape for kit SKUs (LAWN_MOWER_KIT)

The kit version adds the battery and charger in-box. Adjust:

- **Title**: append "with 4.0Ah Battery and Charger" (or whatever the kit ships).
- **Paragraph 2** can reference battery runtime: "the included 4.0Ah battery
  delivers around 45 minutes of cutting."
- **Paragraph 3**: drop the "battery and charger sold separately" line.
- **Bullets**: swap the "shares 40V batteries" bullet for the actual
  in-box capacity.

## Building the same shape for petrol mowers

Different category (not yet mapped to a Kingfisher hierarchy as of 2026-05-19).
When you write a petrol mower exemplar:

- Lead with the same "what gets done" angle: area, terrain, grass length.
- Specify engine type (e.g. "Honda GCV170 4-stroke engine") rather than
  generic "powerful petrol engine".
- Don't compare to electric. Don't apologise for being petrol. The petrol
  buyer chose petrol on purpose.
- Mention fuel type once: "runs on standard unleaded; no oil-mix".
