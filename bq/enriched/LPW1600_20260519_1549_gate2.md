## Body Copy
Required: yes
Value:
Strip moss off patios, rinse the family car, and shift winter grime from garden furniture without committing to a semi-professional rig. The Spectrum LPW1600 delivers up to 130 bar of working pressure and 420 L/hr of flow from a 1600W mains motor. That gives you the cleaning power for the two-or-three-times-a-year jobs most gardens need.

What separates this pressure washer from budget supermarket alternatives sits under the bonnet. A brass pump head and ceramic-coated pistons sit at the heart of the LPW1600, components normally reserved for higher-end machines. That build quality is what supports the 5-year Spectrum warranty (T&C's apply). Most rivals in this price band cap out at 12 to 24 months.

At 5.8kg, the LPW1600 lifts one-handed and rolls between jobs on integrated wheels. A transport handle takes care of hauls over kerbs or up garage steps. A rotating cord holder takes the frustration out of packing the mains cable away. On-board storage clips keep the lance, hose and connections tidy between jobs.

Variable pressure and flow-rate control means easing back for paintwork and bodywork, then dialling up for moss-stripping and patio prep. The 5m steel-braided high-pressure hose pairs with a 5m mains cable for a 10m working radius without extension leads. A 50 degrees Celsius water inlet rating lets you draw from heated supplies when the cold tap won't reach.
