## Body Copy
Required: yes
Value:
Strip moss, algae and ground-in grime off paving, decking, brickwork and vehicles with the Spectrum LPW2200. A 2200W mains motor drives 170 bar of working pressure and 450 litres per hour of flow rate, putting it in the semi-professional bracket while still running off a standard 13A socket.

Build is where most washers at this price-point fall short. The LPW2200 runs a brass pump head and ceramic-coated pistons in place of the plastic internals that fail after a season of heavy use, and it carries a five-year Spectrum warranty as standard (T&Cs apply). The 9kg chassis sits on integrated wheels with a transport handle, so moving it between jobs is a matter of pulling rather than lifting.

Three brushes ship in the box - flat, fixed and rotary - alongside a built-in detergent tank for soap-fed work like car bodywork or decking restoration. Variable pressure and flow control lets the same machine handle paintwork-safe rinses and block-paving deep cleans. The 5m steel-braided hose and 5m mains cable give roughly a 10m working radius without extension leads, and a rotating cord holder ends the unwind-rewind shuffle. Inlet water up to 50 degrees C is supported for warm-water cleaning where needed.
