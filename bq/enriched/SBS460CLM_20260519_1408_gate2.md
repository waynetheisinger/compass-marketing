## Body Copy

Value:
Mow lawns up to 800 m² between charges with the Spectrum SBS460CLM. A brushless motor pairs with two 40V batteries for steady cutting power, even in damp morning grass, and the self-propelled transmission keeps your shoulders relaxed across longer sessions. Five-year warranty as standard.

The 46cm steel deck handles tough grass that defeats the plastic shrouds on cheaper mowers. Seven cutting heights from 25mm to 75mm shift via a single central lever - scalp-close in early spring, longer through the dry summer weeks. Four ways to deal with clippings: collect into the 60-litre bag (with a fill indicator to stop the surprise overflow), mulch back into the lawn, or discharge rear or side. Ball-bearing wheels - 7" front, 10" rear - shrug off uneven ground and resist turf damage.

Bare tool only. Pair with any Spectrum 40V battery: the SBS20CB (2.0Ah, lighter sessions) or the SBS40CB (4.0Ah, longer runtime with steady voltage under load). Charging options: SBSCBC for overnight, SBSCSC for fast single-bay top-ups, or SBSCDC if you rotate two batteries through one charger. Shares the same 40V platform as the wider Spectrum cordless range including hedge trimmers, pole trimmers, and leaf blower vacs.
